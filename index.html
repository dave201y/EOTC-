Welcome to Gibi Gubae

We're a student community carrying one of the oldest living Christian traditions in the world onto MNSU's campus — Ge'ez chant, incense, fasting, and fellowship, open to anyone curious about the Oriental Orthodox faith, whether you grew up in it or have never heard of it before.

What is Oriental Orthodoxy?

Oriental Orthodoxy is a family of ancient Christian churches — Ethiopian, Eritrean, Coptic, Armenian, Syriac, and Malankara among them — that trace their roots to the earliest centuries of the Church. The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church is one of the oldest, with Christianity reaching Ethiopia in the 4th century.

Ancient and apostolic

Our worship, feasts, and church calendar carry forward practice that has continued, largely unbroken, for over 1,600 years.

Ge'ez liturgy

Services are chanted in Ge'ez, the church's historic liturgical language, alongside Amharic and English so newcomers can follow along.

A life of fasting and feast

Our calendar moves through seasons of fasting and celebration together — a rhythm that shapes community life, not just individual devotion.

Come as you are

You don't need to know the prayers, the language, or the customs to come through the door. Most of us are still learning too. We gather twice a week during the semester — no RSVP needed.
