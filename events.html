When we gather

Two weekly meetings, every week of the semester. No RSVP needed — just show up.

WedWEEKLY

Bible Study — 7:00 PM

We work through scripture together, discuss it in plain terms, and leave room for questions. Held at Jacobites Lounge.

ThuWEEKLY

Worship — 8:00 PM

Evening worship on campus, chanted in Ge'ez and Amharic with English so newcomers can follow along.

First time coming?

Wear whatever's comfortable. You're welcome to simply sit and observe — no one will expect you to know the prayers.

Room and location changes

Exact rooms can shift week to week. The current location is always posted on our Telegram channel before each meeting.

Feast day gatherings

Around major feasts on the church calendar, we sometimes add a special gathering outside the weekly schedule — announced on Telegram and Instagram.
